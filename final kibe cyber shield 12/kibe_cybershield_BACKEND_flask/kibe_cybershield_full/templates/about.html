Our Story

About Kibe CyberShield

Born in Nakuru, built for Africa. We protect the digital future of businesses, governments, and individuals across East Africa and beyond.

Our Mission

Securing Africa's Digital Economy

Kibe CyberShield Technologies Ltd was founded with a clear purpose: to make enterprise-grade cybersecurity accessible and affordable for African organisations of all sizes.

From startups in Nairobi to government agencies in Nakuru, hospitals in Mombasa to NGOs in Kisumu — we believe every organisation deserves world-class protection, regardless of budget or technical capacity.

Our team of certified security professionals brings together expertise in threat intelligence, digital forensics, cloud security, and AI-powered defence systems — all purpose-built for the African threat landscape.
